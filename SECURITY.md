# Security Policy

The Stylo team and community take security issues in the Stylo Framework seriously. To report a security issue, fill out the form at [https://erpnext.com/security/report](https://erpnext.com/security/report).

You can help us make Stylo and consequently all Stylo dependent apps like [ERPNext](https://erpnext.com) more secure by following the [Reporting guidelines](https://erpnext.com/security).

We appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose your findings. We'll endeavor to respond quickly, and will keep you updated throughout the process.
